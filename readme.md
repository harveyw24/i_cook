# i_cook

**i_cook** is a meal recommendation tool designed to help users decide what to cook based on the ingredients they currently have on hand. By inputting available ingredients, users receive personalized meal suggestions that match their pantry and fridge contents, making meal planning easier and reducing food waste.

## Recipe Sources

- [JustOneCookbook.com](https://www.justonecookbook.com) (current source)

Additional recipe sources may be added in the future to expand the variety and diversity of meal recommendations.
